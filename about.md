# Auto Deco

Decorate your levels in one click.

Open the editor, pause, press **Auto Deco**, and pick a style:
**Hell, Heaven, Water, Forest, Space, Neon, Candy, Sunset, Ice, Toxic**, a **Random** pick,
or **Custom** with your own object IDs.

- Decorates exposed block tops, sides and bottoms
- Optional background / ground recoloring
- Random sizes for variety
- No Touch option so decorations never hurt
- One Undo reverts everything
